About

I'm a Ph.D. student in Operations and Decision Systems at Université Laval in Quebec City. My research focuses on robust optimization methods for emergency department staff rostering.

Before starting my doctorate, I worked as a researcher at CIRRELT, developing optimization models for transportation networks using MILP, constraint programming, and metaheuristic algorithms.

I hold a M.Sc. in Industrial Engineering from Tarbiat Modares University and a B.Sc. from Ferdowsi University of Mashhad. My work sits at the intersection of operations research, machine learning, and healthcare logistics.

Research Interests

  • Robust & stochastic optimization
  • Healthcare operations & staff scheduling
  • Machine learning for decision support
  • Transportation & logistics optimization
  • Process mining
